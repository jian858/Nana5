Your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Murmur collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, username, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Profile information: profile photo, bio, birthday, school, location, interests and other details you choose to provide.
  • Content and activity: posts, comments, stories, reels, friend connections, chats, notes and notifications.
  • Visitor information: a random first-party browser ID, page path and visit timestamps used for unique visitor counts and service analytics. The visitor tracker does not request your password or email address.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser and connection information processed by service providers to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and sign-in session.
  • Store and display content you choose to share.
  • Deliver messages, notifications and password reset emails.
  • Protect accounts, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors and improve Murmur.
  • Meet legal obligations.

3. Storage and service providers

Murmur uses Supabase for authentication, database services, realtime updates and file storage. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes the sign-in request under its own privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information.

4. What other users can see

Your profile information and content may be visible according to the audience settings and features you use. Messages and private account information are intended to be available only to authorized participants. Do not post information you do not want others to see.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information while needed to provide Murmur, meet legal obligations and protect the service. You may request account deletion through the app or support. Some security and audit records may be retained where required.

6. Your choices

You may update profile information, control what you share, and request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Contact Murmur support through the app for help.

7. Security

Murmur uses Supabase authentication and database access controls, including Row Level Security, to protect account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and use a unique password.

8. Children

Murmur is not intended for children under 13. Contact support if you believe a child under 13 has created an account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when changes are made.
