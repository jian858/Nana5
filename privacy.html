Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Murmur collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: first, middle and last name, username, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Birthday: used to check that you are at least 13 years old.
  • Optional profile details: gender, location, school, phone number, bio, interests and profile or cover photos, if provided.

Posts, stories, comments, likes, friend connections, chats, reactions and notifications are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. Uploaded photos and videos may be stored in Firebase Storage. Browser storage may retain limited preferences and cached app state.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and enable sign-in.
  • To send necessary emails, including password-reset links.
  • To provide social features and display profile information.
  • To protect Murmur, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To comply with applicable law.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. Murmur uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Storage. Supabase is used to create and privately link a separate Supabase account ID to a Firebase account. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or to protect rights and safety.

4. What other people can see

Your name, username and the posts you share are visible to other Murmur users. Think before posting personal details.

5. Storage and retention

Firebase stores account and social information, while Supabase holds a private account-ID link. Providers may process information outside your country. Information is retained while needed to operate the service. Deletion requests should cover the Firebase account, related database records and uploaded files, and the private Supabase account-ID link. Some records may remain where required by law or for security.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to request a copy, correction or deletion of your information, or object to or limit certain uses. Contact Murmur support through the app. We may need to verify your identity.

7. Security

We use trusted providers and reasonable safeguards. No online service is completely secure. Choose a strong, unique password and keep it private.

8. Children

Murmur is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact Murmur support.

9. Cookies and changes

We use limited browser storage for sign-in and settings. See the Cookie Policy. We may update this policy and revise its date when changes are made.
