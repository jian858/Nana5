Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Murmur uses essential browser storage to keep your account session and basic preferences working.

1. Essential storage

  • Supabase Auth session: keeps you signed in securely and refreshes your session.
  • Visitor ID: a random ID stored in local browser storage to count a browser profile once in visitor statistics.
  • Interface preferences: may remember settings such as theme or display preferences.

2. Analytics and advertising

Murmur's first-party visitor counter records a random visitor ID, page path and visit time. It is used for service statistics. Murmur does not use advertising cookies.

3. Managing browser storage

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing it may sign you out, reset preferences and cause your browser to receive a new visitor ID.

4. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
