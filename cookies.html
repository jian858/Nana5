Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Murmur uses cookies and similar technologies, such as browser local storage and IndexedDB.

1. What Murmur uses

Murmur uses storage that is necessary to run the service and remember limited settings. Examples include:

  • murmur-state — limited interface state and cached app data.
  • firebaseLocalStorageDb — Firebase Authentication sign-in state.
  • murmur-cookie-notice — remembers whether the cookie notice was seen.

2. What Murmur does not use

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, according to the current app configuration.

3. Third parties

Murmur uses Google Firebase for authentication and profile storage. Google services may process technical information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy.

4. Managing storage

You can delete or block cookies and site data in browser settings. Doing so may sign you out, reset settings or prevent sign-in from working.

5. Changes and contact

If Murmur adds non-essential cookies or tools, this policy will be updated and permission will be requested where required by law. For questions, contact Murmur support through the app.
